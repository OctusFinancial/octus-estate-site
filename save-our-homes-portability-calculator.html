How portability works

Your savings

Save Our Homes limits how fast a homestead's assessed value can rise, so over time it falls below the home's market value. That gap is your assessment difference.

Moving up or the same

If the new home's just value is equal to or higher than the old home's, the full difference can transfer, up to $500,000.

Downsizing

If the new home is worth less, a proportional share transfers. A home worth 80% as much carries about 80% of the difference.

It isn't automatic

File a homestead application (Form DR-501) and the portability form (Form DR-501T) with the new county's property appraiser, generally by March 1.

The three-year window

The new homestead has to be established on or before January 1 of the third year after leaving the old one.

Within Florida only

Portability moves between Florida counties, not to another state. Couples and co-owners have extra rules, such as Form DR-501TS for splitting a jointly owned home's difference.

This calculator gives an educational estimate only. The $500,000 cap is current as of September 2026; proposals to change it have been considered, so the current limit is worth confirming.
